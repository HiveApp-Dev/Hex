Hex Research Hub

Preserving Human Knowledge in the Age of AI

Knowledge survives when it is copied, verified, taught, and shared.

Hex Research Hub exists to help preserve educational and scientific knowledge by continuously collecting, organizing, and explaining information from public sources before valuable information disappears behind paywalls, link rot, platform shutdowns, content deletions, or future AI-generated noise.

⸻

Why Hex Exists

Throughout history, humanity has lost enormous amounts of knowledge:

* The Library of Alexandria
* Burned manuscripts and archives
* Lost scientific records
* Deleted websites and digital resources
* Broken academic links
* Paywalled research inaccessible to the public

Today, a new challenge exists.

As AI-generated content floods the internet, original sources become harder to find. Researchers have documented concerns around:

* Link rot and disappearing web content
* Dataset contamination
* AI models referencing AI-generated material
* Declining visibility of primary sources
* Academic preservation challenges

Hex was built to fight that trend.

Instead of generating endless recycled content, Hex focuses on:

✅ Finding original sources
✅ Preserving educational information
✅ Creating human-readable summaries
✅ Maintaining citations and provenance
✅ Organizing knowledge into searchable research libraries

⸻

What Hex Does

Hex Research Hub is a fully autonomous research system that continuously discovers, collects, organizes, and explains educational and scientific knowledge.

Sources include:

* Wikipedia
* OpenAlex
* Crossref
* Semantic Scholar
* arXiv

No API keys required.

No proprietary databases required.

Everything is built around publicly accessible educational and scientific information.
